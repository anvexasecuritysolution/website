# Anvexa Security Solutions: Business Model and Approach

Prepared on 18 October 2026 from the current website and the founder's instructions in this conversation.

This document describes Anvexa's positioning and proposed operating model. It does not establish existing revenue, customers, certifications, assessment capacity or round-the-clock coverage. Commercial arrangements and service availability still need to be confirmed for each engagement.

## 1. What Anvexa Does

**Anvexa is a Noida-based cybersecurity startup serving Indian MSMEs and startups through scoped security assessments, remediation guidance and agreed retesting.**

The initial focus is **Web Applications, APIs, Servers and Networks**. The purpose is to help a business understand its weaknesses, decide what to fix first and verify the agreed changes.

The customer proposition is:

> Find Security Gaps. Fix What Matters.

Anvexa's core offer is a professional service engagement. The illustrative dashboard and report format on the website explain possible outputs; they are not evidence of a production SaaS platform or completed customer work.

### Likely Buyers

- Founders and business owners seeking a practical starting point.
- CTOs, engineering managers and IT heads responsible for remediation.
- Security, compliance and procurement teams reviewing vendors or audit evidence.

## 3. The Customer Problem

An assessment report alone does not resolve a weakness. A growing business may need help answering:

1. Which systems should we assess first?
2. Which findings represent a meaningful business risk?
3. What should our developers or IT team change?
4. Who owns each action, and when should it be completed?
5. Have the fixes actually resolved the findings?
6. What can we show management, a customer or an independent auditor?

Anvexa's opportunity is to connect **Testing, Risk Prioritisation, Remediation Guidance and Verification** in a clearly defined engagement.

### Compliance Positioning

The website discusses **CERT-In, the DPDP Act, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS and SEBI/RBI/IRDAI requirements** to explain relevant customer needs.

Anvexa's stated role is to support technical readiness and evidence. Mentioning these frameworks does not establish accreditation, CERT-In empanelment, the ability to issue a SOC 2 report, PCI assessor status or authority to certify an organisation. Applicability and required assessor credentials must be confirmed before accepting a specialised engagement.

## 5. How the Business Earns Revenue

The website supports a **service-led model with an assessment entry offer and a recurring-review path**. No fixed public price, established margin or revenue forecast has been agreed.

| Revenue stream | Offer | Commercial basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-Time Security Assessment | A defined application or infrastructure assessment with agreed outputs and retest terms | Custom project quote |
| Recurring Security Reviews | Scheduled or release-driven reassessment, remediation follow-up and reporting | Custom agreement with a defined cadence and asset scope |
| Specialist Advisory or Response Support | Scope-specific monitoring, incident support or advisory work | Separate agreement after confirming availability and responsibilities |
| Development Support | Requirements coordinated through Anvexa with Benkyō Technologies | Commercial terms to be agreed; no referral fee, revenue share or margin is established here |

### Quote Factors

- Applications, API endpoints, hosts and network ranges included.
- User roles, tenant models, integrations and business-logic complexity.
- External versus authenticated access and any source-code review.
- Environment constraints, testing windows and coordination effort.
- Reporting, handover, retesting and evidence-mapping requirements.
- Delivery schedule, specialist support and direct partner/tool costs.

### Recommended Cost Discipline

Before setting a project fee, estimate tester time, technical review, reporting, client coordination and retesting. Compare the estimate with actual effort after delivery.

```text
Project contribution =
  Collected project revenue
  - Direct delivery labour
  - Direct tool and partner costs
  - Project-specific acquisition costs

Operating result =
  Total project contribution
  - Fixed operating costs
```

Count each cost once. Include founder delivery time even if it is not yet paid as a salary. These formulas are planning tools, not evidence that Anvexa currently earns a profit.

Payment milestones, taxes, cancellation terms, change requests and additional retest fees remain commercial decisions to document in the proposal or agreement.

## 6. The Delivery Approach

The detailed service lifecycle is delivered through four practical stages.

### Stage 1: Agree the Scope

- Discuss the business goal and systems involved.
- Confirm ownership or permission to test, access requirements and exclusions.
- Agree the testing environment, window, escalation contact and stop conditions.
- Confirm confidentiality, evidence handling, outputs, fees and retest terms in writing.

**Result:** An authorised engagement with understood boundaries.

### Stage 2: Assess and Prioritise

- Discover the assets within scope.
- Carry out the agreed technical checks and validate findings.
- Explain the evidence, affected systems, likely impact and testing limitations.
- Rank findings so the client knows what needs attention first.

**Result:** An evidence-based view of the assessed risks.

### Stage 3: Support Remediation

- Walk the client's developers or IT team through the findings.
- Recommend fixes and agree responsible owners and target dates.
- Record unresolved issues and any agreed interim safeguards.
- Treat implementation or additional development as separate work when necessary.

**Result:** A practical action plan rather than an unexplained report.

### Stage 4: Verify and Hand Over

- Retest the agreed fixes within the contracted window.
- Distinguish resolved findings from open issues and limitations.
- Hand over the management summary, technical findings and verification records.
- Discuss future assessment needs without implying that continuous coverage is included.

**Result:** A documented position at the end of the engagement and a clear next step.

### Operating Safeguards

Testing must remain authorised and within scope. Production work can affect availability; agree safeguards rather than promising zero disruption. Use minimum necessary access, an agreed secure sharing method and test data where practical. Define report recipients, retention and access revocation before handover.

## 7. Customer Acquisition and Sales

### Website Journey

```text
Visitor understands the offer
  → Checks coverage and application relevance
  → Reviews the team, delivery process and illustrative report
  → Selects “Discuss an Assessment”
  → Submits an enquiry on Contact
  → Discusses scope
  → Receives a written proposal
  → Authorises the work
```

The Contact form requires **First Name, Email and Phone**. Other details help qualification without making the first enquiry unnecessarily difficult. Company and industry remain available in an optional section. Assessment links preselect the relevant service.

### Recommended Qualification Questions

1. What business event prompted the assessment: launch, customer review, release, audit preparation or a suspected issue?
2. Which applications, APIs, servers or networks are involved?
3. Who can authorise testing and provide access?
4. Is there a deadline, required report format or assessor eligibility condition?
5. Who will implement fixes and participate in retesting?
6. Does the request match available capability and delivery capacity?

### Recommended Acquisition Channels

- Clear service pages and practical articles answering buyer questions.
- Professional visibility through genuine team profiles and useful educational content.
- Relevant relationships with software agencies and IT service providers.
- Referrals from satisfied customers when actual delivery evidence exists.
- Focused conversations with the initial target segments, followed by properly scoped paid work.

These are proposed channels, not claims that an established lead pipeline exists. Validate demand through qualified enquiries, accepted proposals and paid delivery rather than website traffic alone.

## 8. Retention and Expansion

Recurring work should follow a real customer need:

- Significant application releases or infrastructure changes.
- Agreed periodic reassessment of important assets.
- Follow-up on unresolved findings and verified fixes.
- Scheduled evidence preparation for customer or independent audit reviews.
- Newly disclosed threats affecting the client's assessed systems.

The recurring agreement should specify assets, cadence, responsibilities, reporting, retest allowances and change-control terms. Repeated assessments are different from continuously staffed monitoring or an incident-response SLA.

Expand into monitoring, MDR, incident response or regulated-sector work only after confirming the people, tools, operating procedures and contractual coverage needed to deliver it.

## 9. Development Partner Model

**Benkyō Technologies** is the development-support partner shown on the website. Support areas include websites, web and mobile apps, custom software, SaaS, AI automation, QA, UI/UX and API integration.

- The customer contacts **Anvexa**, not the partner directly through the website.
- Anvexa coordinates the requirement and the next discussion.
- Development scope, delivery ownership, charges, data sharing and support responsibilities need agreement before work starts.
- Security assessment and development implementation remain separately defined activities.

The partnership must not be used to imply Anvexa owns Benkyō's products, credentials, staff or completed projects. No exclusivity or commercial revenue split has been confirmed.

## 11. Trust and Brand Approach

The founder has confirmed that approved customer testimonials, completed assessment evidence and team certifications are not currently available for publication.

### Evidence Available on the Website

- Named team profiles and supplied LinkedIn links.
- A clearly labelled illustrative report format.
- Defined assessment coverage, delivery stages and service boundaries.
- Contact details, Privacy Policy, Terms and consent controls.

These help a buyer evaluate Anvexa, but they are not equivalent to independent proof of delivery outcomes.

### Evidence to Add When Available

- Verifiable qualifications and certifications with accurate ownership and validity.
- Customer-approved, anonymised case studies with scope, actions and limitations.
- Redacted completed report examples where disclosure is permitted.
- Genuine testimonials and client logos with permission.
- Measured results that can be supported by engagement records.

Avoid invented reviews, customer counters, breach-prevention guarantees, accreditation claims and stock imagery presented as real customers. The site should retain its navy/cyan identity, consistent typography, clear cards and purposeful animation while keeping the service easy to understand.

## 13. Practical Growth Sequence

| Phase | Focus | Evidence needed before expanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish the offer | Repeatable web/API/server/network assessment scopes and clear proposals | Customers understand the offer and agree to paid work |
| Prove delivery | Consistent reports, remediation discussions and retests | Actual delivery hours, quality review and customer feedback |
| Earn repeat work | Release-driven or scheduled reassessment | A recurring client need and deliverable scope that can be fulfilled |
| Build proof | Approved case studies and verified team credentials | Permission and supporting records |
| Expand carefully | More complex environments, fintech/lending or specialist operations | Relevant capability, eligibility, capacity and contractual arrangements |

This sequence is a proposed operating approach. It is not a promise of a launch date, a fixed timeline or a customer/revenue target.
